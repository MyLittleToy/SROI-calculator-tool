SROI Mathematical Proof Framework

This implementation maps individual financial configurations precisely onto standard accounting conventions from the HM Treasury Green Book.

Step 1: Gross Social Value Generation

Financial baseline values are aligned directly with external indicators found within the Manchester Unit Cost Database.

Gross Value = Participants × Financial Proxy Value

Step 2: Net Social Value Conversion Matrix

After Deadweight = Gross Value × (1 − Deadweight %)
After Attribution = After Deadweight × (1 − Attribution %)
Net Value = After Attribution × (1 − Displacement %)

Step 3: Multi-Year Future Value Discounting Loop

For each year (y = 0 to benefit_period − 1):
  Year Value = Net Value × (1 − Drop-off)^y
  Discounted Value = Year Value ÷ (1 + Discount Rate)^y
Present Value = Σ Discounted values across all model years

Step 4: Social Return on Investment Quotient

SROI Ratio = Total Present Value ÷ Total Investment Amount

SROI Assumptions Reference Guide

Deadweight

The proportion of the social outcome that would have occurred anyway, even if our project or intervention had never run.

Context: A certain percentage of young people would have found employment or improved their life skills independently via existing support systems or family networks.
Attribution

An assessment of how much of the achieved outcome was actually driven or caused by the contribution of other concurrent services, organisations, or individuals.

Context: A young person's success might be partly due to college coursework, school advisors, or parallel healthcare services alongside our mentoring programmes.
Displacement

An assessment of whether the project's positive outcome unintentionally displaced or cancelled out other social benefits or activities elsewhere in the community.

Context: Helping a young person secure a highly competitive local job vacancy might mean another local applicant did not get it, shifting the unemployment elsewhere.
Annual Drop-off

The rate at which the long-term benefit or positive impact of our intervention diminishes over subsequent years for the individual participant.

Context: The confidence, skill boost, or positive lifestyle trajectory gained from our training might fade naturally year-on-year if a participant moves into a new environment.
Discount Rate

The percentage reduction applied to social values generated in future years to calculate their equivalence in today's terms, adjusting for the time-value of social impact.

Context: This calculator defaults to a 3.5% discount rate in accordance with standard HM Treasury Green Book accounting rules for public or charity evaluations.
Calculation Period

The fixed horizon of years over which we are actively tracking, modeling, and projecting the ongoing impacts generated by a specific cohort or funding investment.

Context: While individual touchpoints might last a single year, the life skills or crime reductions track forward across multiple years to evaluate structural long-term reform.
